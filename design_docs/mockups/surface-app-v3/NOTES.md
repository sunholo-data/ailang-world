# Tauri v3: a timeline you can move through, written for a person

- **Added:** a time axis you can pan and zoom, with five levels: Close, Steps (the v2 spacing and the default), Hours, Days and Weeks. Steps gives each merge its own slot. Further out, merges sit at their real times. The 44-day quiet gap before 16 Oct is drawn as an axis break. Dense runs collapse into count bubbles; clicking a bubble or zooming in opens it up. Labels thin out as you zoom out, and the axis changes from clock times to hours, then days, then weeks. A strip along the bottom shows the whole history in brane colours, with a window over the part in view. The control bar shows the visible range (for example "Sat 17 Oct 11:06 → Tue 20 Oct 11:39 · 28 h before now"), ‹ ›, Now, and − Steps +.
- **Navigation:** ← and → move a page. Shift+← and Shift+→ move one state along a lane, and ↑ and ↓ change lanes. End or "Now" eases back to the newest state; Home goes to the oldest. + and − zoom around the focused state. To pan, drag the graph, use a horizontal scroll or trackpad swipe, or use a plain mouse wheel. ⌘ or Ctrl with the scroll wheel, or a trackpad pinch, zooms around the pointer. In the overview strip, drag the window to move or click to jump. Click, Enter and Esc still zoom into a brane and back out.
- **Human first:** nodes are labelled with shortened PR titles; rig bookkeeping such as "(through World, w189c)" is removed. Merges into the shared world are small unlabelled dots that show their date and time when you point at one. Hovering a step shows its title, the agent's own one-sentence summary, the agent and model, graders (first round ✓ or the number of rounds), agent-minutes, the relative and exact time, and then the episode, PR and hash in small mono type.
- **Lane headers:** each shows the brane's name, its goal (truncated; the full text is in a hover card), its progress (row 188 as a milestone track, "6 of 9 · M4a in review · next M4b"; the others as "N steps landed"), and active or quiet.
- **Now card:** a Today block reads "3 steps landed · Claude 1, Codex 2 · 1 first-round pass · 3 fix rounds · 25 agent-minutes". Running reads "w188g · World os surface".
- **Brane card:** the agent's summary sits under the headline. The task id has moved into Inputs. Checks opens with "Passed all 8 checks on the first round · 3.5 agent-minutes". The rail and the earlier and later bars show titles, and other rail states now show their summary, agent and checks. Source chips open with the step's title on hover, and the address hint goes away inside a brane.
- **Fit:** at 932 px the last bar still ends at y=923 (check-brane-932.png). The app is 4.6 MB; it ran for 5 s at about 100 MB RSS and was killed by its pid.
- **Still mechanical, and why:** crumbs and the address field use addresses (`row-189@6`) because they are the typed language. Bars and rail rows keep episode ids and PR numbers as handles beside the titles. Some titles are still mechanics, such as "Row 156: V25 re-measure (v0.54.15 …)", because those are the PR titles. Some agent summaries are thin, such as "Implemented M1. Added optional serve …", because that is the agent's own text. Quiet branes share one lane. Only row 189 opens as a brane, and the inputs, decisions and reads for every step except w189e are not in this data. Running maps w188g to its lane from the episode prefix. For w188e, data with firstRoundPass and 2 rounds shows "first round ✓". The shots come from headless Chrome; the native WKWebView render was not pixel-checked.
